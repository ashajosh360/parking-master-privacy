Parking Master Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026. Applies to the Android 0.1.0 closed-test release.

Who we are

Parking Master is a parking puzzle game provided by joshua360, the developer name used on Google Play. For privacy questions, contact ashaolujoshua360@gmail.com.

Information used by this test version

The game saves puzzle progress, virtual coins, garage selections, quest progress, and your settings locally on your device so you can continue playing offline. This release does not require an account or provide cloud saving. Its virtual coins have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or transferred.

The developer has not connected this Android test release to a production analytics or gameplay server. The optional gameplay and performance sharing preferences do not currently upload information from this Android release. We do not request your name, phone number, contacts, financial information, or installed-app list in the game. Advertising identifiers are not requested by the developer's game code in this release.

Optional permissions

- Notifications: if you enable reminders and grant Android's notification permission, the app schedules a local puzzle reminder. You can turn reminders off in the game or Android settings.

- Approximate location: the test app contains an optional permission control for planned location-related features. This release does not retrieve or transmit location coordinates. You may deny or revoke the permission without affecting the puzzles.

- Network access: Google Play may be contacted to check for app updates. Core puzzle play and local progress saving work without a connection.

Advertising and platform services

Advertising is disabled in this closed-test release. Unity LevelPlay and the Unity Ads adapter are included for future integration, but the game does not initialize them or request advertisements with the current configuration. The developer will update this policy and the app's disclosures before enabling advertisements or new data collection.

Google Play and Android may process installation, update, security, or diagnostic information according to your platform settings and Google's own policies. Information handled by those services is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Future Unity advertising use will also require the applicable Unity Privacy Policy and in-app privacy choices.

Sharing and international processing

The developer does not sell local game saves or send them to advertisers in this test release. Local saves are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. If you contact us by email, your message and reply address are handled by our email provider, which may process them in other countries. Do not email passwords, payment details, or sensitive documents when reporting a game issue.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you delete it. Use Settings > Delete my saved data in the game, clear the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored progress. Deleting progress is permanent unless you separately kept a copy. There is no game account to delete in this release.

Privacy/support correspondence is retained only while needed to respond and resolve the request. You may ask us to delete that correspondence by emailing the privacy contact above; email-provider backup or operational retention may continue according to that provider's practices.

Intended audience

Parking Master is intended for people 13 years and older, and is not directed to children under 13. We do not ask players to submit personal profiles. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support email, contact us so we can address the request.

Security and changes

Keep your device protected and install updates through Google Play. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. If the app later adds live ads, cloud saving, online analytics, or other features that change its use of information, we will update this policy and provide any required notices or choices before those features collect additional information.

Delete your Ludo Arena account

Developer: joshua360 · Updated 24 October 2026

Request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data. You do not need to reinstall Ludo Arena.

Request account deletion by email

If the button does not open an email app, email ashaolujoshua360@gmail.com with subject Ludo Arena account deletion request. Send from the email used to sign in. Do not send passwords, login codes or payment information.

What happens next

  1. We verify ownership using your sign-in email. Contact support at the same address if you have lost access to it.
  2. We process verified requests within 30 days and confirm completion by email. This permanently deletes the account; it is not deactivation.
  3. We remove the Firebase sign-in account, account/provider links, sessions, refresh credentials, notification bindings/tokens/history, tournament entries and account-linked command records. Shared match/tournament records are removed or anonymized so they no longer link to your account.

Watching data

Account deletion also removes your watching permissions and related command receipts and buffered match snapshots. Shared game records are removed or anonymized. Frames already viewed by spectators cannot be recalled.

Retained and local data

Limited records may remain where necessary for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations; we explain applicable exceptions in our response. Provider backups may persist temporarily under provider schedules. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local guest saves, settings and practice rewards. Uninstalling alone does not delete an online account.

No payments or subscriptions need cancellation in this closed test. To request deletion of particular personal data while keeping your account, email the same address and describe the data.

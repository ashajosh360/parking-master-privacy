Ludo Arena privacy policy

Developer: joshua360 · Updated 23 October 2026 · Android: com.joshua360.ludoarena

Request account and associated data deletion

Offline play

Guest play works without an account. Preferences, offline saves, practice rewards and a random installation ID are stored on your device. This is not a hardware identifier. Device model, operating system and language are cached locally for compatibility. Offline saves and rewards are not uploaded or converted into online money. Android app backup is disabled.

Optional accounts and online play

Firebase Authentication processes email/password or Google sign-in, email, provider profile information where supplied, user identifiers and security information such as IP addresses. Our identity service stores provider/user links and revocable sessions. Refresh credentials use encrypted device storage. Online servers process account identifiers, room membership, tournament entries, dice/move commands, game state and results. Other participants see occupied seats and shared game state.

Notifications

With permission, Firebase Cloud Messaging and our service process installation identifiers, delivery tokens, account/session IDs, Android platform, app version, language, notification content and delivery status. Disable notifications in Android settings. Signing out disables the installation's account delivery binding.

Providers and security

Google/Firebase, Render and Neon process information for authentication, hosting, database storage, security and delivery. Firebase SDKs process app/device metadata to operate their services. Hosting providers may process IP addresses and operational logs. Connections use HTTPS. We do not sell personal data or integrate advertising or analytics SDKs. Google Play and Android separately process downloads, updates and platform diagnostics under their policies.

Retention and deletion

Account and online data are retained while needed for your account and features. Request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data without reinstalling the app. We verify ownership and process requests within 30 days. Shared match records may retain an anonymous former-player identifier. Limited security records and provider backups may remain temporarily where necessary; we explain any applicable exception. Uninstalling alone does not delete an online account. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local guest data.

Closed-test scope

For adults aged 18 and older. No real-money payments, wagering, cash prizes, withdrawals, advertising, chat, microphone/video access or geolocation tracking are enabled. Practice rewards have no monetary value. Eligible updates use Google Play with your consent; executable code is not silently replaced.

Ludo Arena privacy policy

Offline test release · Updated 20 October 2026 · Developer: joshua360

This notice covers the offline Android test app Ludo Arena, package com.joshua360.ludoarena.

Information stored on your device

The app stores settings, your dice preference and one offline game so you can resume it. This test build does not require an account and does not transmit this information to our servers or share it with advertisers. Android backup may back up app data under your device account and settings.

Features in this test

No advertising, analytics SDK, payments, withdrawals, chat, microphone access, location tracking or remote notifications are enabled. Bundled sound effects do not use your microphone. Offline results cannot credit money or an online wallet. The test is intended for adults aged 18 and older.

Deletion

Start a new offline game to replace its saved progress. To remove all local settings and saved data, clear Ludo Arena's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Your platform backup settings may retain or restore backups; manage those through your platform account.

Platform services

Google Play and your operating system may process downloads, crash reports and platform diagnostics under their own privacy policies. We have not integrated third-party advertising or analytics SDKs into this build.

Future features

Online accounts, tournaments, communications, regional eligibility and payments are planned separately. This notice and any required permissions will be updated before those features are activated.

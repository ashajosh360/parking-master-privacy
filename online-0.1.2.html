Parking Master Privacy Policy

18 October 2026. Applies to Android 0.1.2. Earlier offline versions 0.1.0–0.1.1 use the offline-build policy.

Who we are

Parking Master is provided by joshua360. Contact ashaolujoshua360@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Local play

Progress, virtual coins, garage selections, quests and settings are saved on your device. You can play offline without an account. Coins have no cash value. Full saves are not uploaded and cloud saving is not provided.

Online services

The app may contact our Cloudflare Workers for remote configuration and validation of completed puzzle moves. These validation sessions use random temporary tokens and expire after one hour. They are not player profiles or a cloud wallet. Cloudflare processes network requests, including IP addresses, as the hosting provider. Application rate limits use salted minute-based IP hashes, separate from analytics. Expired buckets are cleaned hourly.

Optional activity and performance reports

These choices start off. If enabled, activity reports contain only numeric level and move counts; performance reports contain only app version and load time. Events do not include your session token or a player identifier. They have seven-day scheduled retention. Because events are not linked to a player identifier, we cannot locate an individual player's submitted numeric event after receipt.

Notifications and permissions

Daily reminders are local Android notifications. Separately enabling Game update notifications uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, which processes a Firebase installation identifier, delivery token, technical delivery information and subscription to the parking-updates topic. Our Cloudflare database does not store push tokens. Remote notifications stay off if Firebase configuration is missing or permission is denied. Turning the choice off requests topic unsubscription and messaging-token deletion. Delivery is not guaranteed and queued messages may be delayed. Android settings can block notifications immediately.

The approximate-location permission control is for a future feature; this version does not retrieve or upload location coordinates. Denying location does not affect puzzles. We do not collect names, contacts, installed-app lists, financial details or advertising IDs in this version.

Advertising and platform services

Ads remain disabled. LevelPlay and the Unity adapter are bundled but not initialized without separate publisher and privacy configuration. Google Play can check for app updates. Google/Firebase services follow the Google Privacy Policy; Cloudflare follows its Privacy Policy. Their infrastructure may process information in other countries. We do not sell game saves or optional reports.

Request deletion

For Parking Master data deletion, first open Settings and turn Game update notifications off to request deletion of the delivery token. Use Delete my saved data to remove local progress and choices. For additional help, email ashaolujoshua360@gmail.com with subject Parking Master data deletion and explain the data you want deleted. No game account exists. Do not send passwords or payment details. Numeric telemetry has no player identifier and expires after seven days, so we cannot locate individual player reports. Validation sessions expire after one hour. Provider security records and backups follow provider retention.

Retention and deletion

Use Settings → Delete my saved data, Android clear storage, or uninstall to remove local progress. Deleting game data also requests remote-notification opt-out when configured. Validation sessions expire automatically; anonymous events and rate buckets follow the cleanup periods above. Cloudflare's platform security/account records follow its own retention. Email support messages remain only while needed to respond and resolve a request; ask us to delete correspondence at the address above, subject to provider backup retention.

Audience, security and changes

The game is intended for ages 13 and above. No account or personal profile is required. Protect your device and install updates through Google Play. No storage or transmission is guaranteed completely secure. We will update policy and in-app choices before introducing new collection or live advertising.

Questions

Email ashaolujoshua360@gmail.com with subject Parking Master privacy.

Optional videos in local Android preview builds (19 October 2026)

The published 0.1.2 build above continues to have ads disabled. New local Android previews can offer optional rewarded videos after a separate session consent choice for adults aged 18 or above. Unity LevelPlay / ironSource Ads and Unity Ads process IP addresses (including approximate location), device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Information can be collected and shared with advertising partners. Personalization consent is set off and the sale/sharing restriction is set on. The app does not request Android advertising-ID permission. Gameplay reporting choices do not enable ads. Base rewards remain free, and under-18 players should keep videos off.

Allow videos applies only to the current app session. Turn rewarded videos off stops new app requests and automatic reloads; close the app to end the existing SDK session. This does not erase provider records already processed. Providers control retention and deletion of their records; see the Unity Privacy Policy and its privacy-rights request options. No ad account or cloud wallet is created for players.

Parking Master Privacy Policy

19 October 2026. Applies to Android 0.1.3. See the 0.1.2 policy or the 0.1.0–0.1.1 offline policy for earlier versions.

Who we are

Parking Master is provided by joshua360. Email ashaolujoshua360@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests.

Offline play and first opening

Puzzles, progress, virtual coins, garage selections and settings are stored on your device. You can play offline without creating a game account. Full saves are not uploaded and cloud saving is not provided. Coins have no cash value. On first opening, a privacy screen explains online features. Optional reporting, notifications and location choices start off. Android asks separately for selected device permissions. Advertising has a separate adult consent choice.

Privacy-choice records

After you continue through the privacy screen, the app records your choices and actual permission status on opening and when they change. Records contain a random installation identifier, random event identifier, policy version, reason and timestamps, plus yes/no choices for activity reports, performance reports, location, reminders, push and advertising. The installation identifier is hashed with a server secret before database storage. Offline records queue on your device, with at most 30 awaiting records, and retry when connected. These functional records are separate from optional gameplay analytics. Records are retained for up to 90 days, then removed by scheduled cleanup. The publisher can view recent records through an owner-only dashboard protected by Cloudflare Access.

Online validation and optional reports

Cloudflare Workers provides remote configuration and puzzle-move validation. Validation uses random temporary session tokens expiring after one hour; these are not player profiles or cloud wallets. Cloudflare processes requests, including IP addresses, as our hosting provider. Application rate limits use salted minute-based IP hashes, cleaned after expiry. Optional activity reports contain numeric level and move counts; optional performance reports contain app version and load time. They exclude player and session identifiers and expire after seven days. We cannot locate a particular player's anonymous numeric report after receipt.

Notifications and location

Daily reminders are local Android notifications. Separately enabling game update notifications uses Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google processes an installation identifier, delivery token, technical delivery information and subscription to the parking-updates topic. Our Cloudflare database does not store push tokens. Turning push off requests topic unsubscription and messaging-token deletion. Android settings can block notifications immediately. Delivery may be delayed or unavailable.

The approximate-location control is reserved for future events. This version does not retrieve or upload location coordinates. Denying permissions does not prevent puzzles. The app does not upload names, contacts or installed-app inventories to our backend.

Advertising

Version 0.1.3 supports banners, interstitials between completed levels and optional rewarded videos through Unity LevelPlay / ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. Ad services initialize only after the separate advertising privacy notice is accepted for the current session by an adult aged 18 or above. Under-18 players should decline ad processing. Puzzles and base rewards remain available when consent is declined or ads are unavailable.

These advertising services may collect and share IP addresses (including inferred approximate location), device identifiers, generated user identifiers, purchase history, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Personalization consent is set off and the sale/sharing restriction is set on. Android advertising-ID permission is removed. Gameplay-report choices do not grant ad consent. Advertising privacy choices in Settings lets you withdraw consent to new ad requests; close the app to end the existing SDK session. Withdrawal does not erase information already processed by providers. See the Unity Privacy Policy and its privacy-rights request options for provider retention and deletion.

Ad-free passes and Google Play

When activated and available in Google Play, paid passes remove banner and interstitial ads for seven or 30 days. They do not renew automatically. Optional rewarded videos remain available. Google Play handles payment details; we do not receive your card or bank information. To verify a pass, the app sends its product identifier and Google Play purchase token to our backend, which verifies and acknowledges the purchase with Google. The database stores a hash of the purchase token, product identifier, expiry, active status and last verification time. The full token is used for verification and is not stored in our database or application logs. Verification records remain until a valid deletion request; Google may retain its own transaction records. Verified expiry is cached on the device for offline use. Purchases require online verification and Google Play availability; disabled purchases cannot charge you.

Google Play may also check for app updates. Google/Firebase processing follows the Google Privacy Policy. Hosting and dashboard sign-in follow the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Providers may process data in other countries. We do not sell game saves or optional reports.

Deletion and your choices

Turn off game update notifications to request delivery-token deletion. Settings → Delete my saved data, Android clear storage or uninstall removes local progress and privacy choices. Local deletion does not automatically remove privacy audit records or verified purchase records from our backend. Privacy records expire after 90 days; anonymous optional reports after seven days; validation sessions after one hour. Scheduled cleanup may take until the next hourly run.

For earlier backend deletion, email ashaolujoshua360@gmail.com with subject Parking Master data deletion. For privacy records, export your progress before deleting local data and provide only its installationId field so we can locate the hashed record. For purchase records, provide the Google Play order reference and product name; we will arrange secure verification if needed. Do not email passwords, payment details or purchase tokens. Deleting a verified purchase record can affect pass restoration; it does not cancel a Google transaction or request a refund. We may retain records where necessary to resolve disputes or meet applicable obligations. Provider security records and backups follow provider policies. Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request; you can request its deletion too.

Audience, security and changes

The game is intended for ages 13 and above; advertising consent in this version is restricted to adults. Network requests use HTTPS. No transmission or storage is guaranteed completely secure. Install updates through Google Play. We will update this policy and relevant choices when collection changes.
